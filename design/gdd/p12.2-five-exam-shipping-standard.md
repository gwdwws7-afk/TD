# P12.2 Five Exam Shipping Standard

## Goal

Apply the L09 presentation slice to L05, L13, L17 and L20 without flattening the five exams into one generic encounter. Every exam must retain the P12.1 animation, audio, concise-feedback and result-chart baseline while exposing a distinct scenario decision and replay lesson.

## Exam Identities

| Level | Identity | Opening | Decision | Failure signature | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L05 | Reserve Window | Depot Clock Live | Wait for delivery or dispatch light | Early Dispatch / Empty Flank | Depot Timetable Held |
| L09 | Switch Commitment | Junction Armed | Divert before dispatch and hold the new lane | Late Switch / Coverage Gap | Switchback Secured |
| L13 | Purge Timing | Kiln Pressure Rising | Break the densest pack and retain exit control | Purge Mistimed / Armor Intact | Kiln Pressure Vented |
| L17 | Phase Preparation | Terminus Echo Online | Expose the elite and cancel one threshold | Matrix Desync / Breaker Unused | Terminus Phase Stabilized |
| L20 | Final Convergence | Final Convergence | Break the 70% and 35% phases and converge on command | Phase Break Missed / Convergence Late | The Last Ember Held |

Each identity owns unique opening, escalation and decision beats. The first Introduce, Reinforce and Exam/Boss phases trigger those beats once per run. Result titles, failure labels and the scenario recommendation reuse the same identity instead of exposing internal configuration keys.

## Map Device Feedback

Every exam creates one non-interactive world device at an authored location. The device has a grounded shadow, state ring, charge pips and an activation pulse. Runtime state is visible without opening a panel:

- Reserve Train shows remaining calls and pulses on dispatch and delivery.
- Canyon Switch cycles the active route pip and pulses when the route changes.
- Kiln Purge shows limited charges and flashes when the battlefield purge fires.
- Phase Breaker expands with Boss phase progression and pulses on use or threshold resolution.
- L20 uses a larger red final-operation state while L17 retains the cyan calibration state.

The dedicated Image 2.0 resource paths live under `Art/Exam/P12`. `tools/generate_p122_exam_devices.ps1` produces the four map-device masters with the user-provided local API key. Existing checked-in industrial sprites are explicit runtime fallbacks, so a failed generation request cannot create missing or empty battlefield objects.

## Feedback Budget

P12.1 feedback limits now apply to every map. Specialization names are reduced to a single tactical token, keeping all floating labels at 14 characters or fewer. Full specialization names remain available in tower and result panels.

## Strategy Gate

The five milestone exams now require all three Standard simulator strategies to win with three distinct formation signatures. The prior two-signature threshold is no longer sufficient. This is a content-diversity gate, not a substitute for the real-time calibration scheduled for P12.4.

## Automated Acceptance

`tools/td_mcp_p122_exam_matrix.ps1` captures three states for each exam:

- 1280x720 combat decision frame.
- 1280x720 result frame.
- 960x540 result frame.

Every result validates the P12.1 baseline plus five-profile uniqueness, scenario type, world-device readiness and clearance, one-shot beat counts, result identity, three successful strategy signatures and text fit. Campaign progress and selected level are restored after each pass.
