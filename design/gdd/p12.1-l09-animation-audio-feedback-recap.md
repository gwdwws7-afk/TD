# P12.1 L09 Presentation Vertical Slice

## Goal

Move L09 from a readable prototype to a production-facing combat slice. The slice must connect unit motion, combat events, sound hierarchy, concise feedback, and post-run analysis without changing route simulation or combat balance.

## Motion Standard

- Enemy roots remain on the authored route. Only the child visual receives stride, lean, and hit squash.
- Enemy sprite sequences use 6-8 authored frames with randomized starts to avoid synchronized marching.
- Tower sprite sequences restart on the real fire event.
- Tower windup compresses the silhouette, fire adds a short recoil, and upgrades add a vertical release pulse.
- Motion amplitude stays below route and placement readability thresholds.

## Audio Standard

Three runtime voices prevent routine hits from cutting tactical or critical cues:

- Routine: hit and build transients.
- Tactical: armor break, slow, specialization, resonance, upgrade, and wave state.
- Critical: leak, boss warning, critical defense, victory, and defeat.

Each cue uses a deterministic layered profile. Metallic events mix short noise and inharmonic partials, control events use a low secondary oscillator, resonance uses harmonic partials, and warnings add a pulsed sub layer.

## Feedback Budget

- Routine hit: value only.
- Tactical state: marker plus value or one short token.
- Critical state: marker plus one short consequence.
- Floating combat labels are capped at 14 active items and 14 characters per item in the P12.1 fixture.
- Cinematic bodies use one decision phrase rather than a repeated sentence.

## Post-Run Charts

The standard run result presents:

- Five tactical axes: coverage, counter, output, economy, and command.
- Up to four lane clear bars with leak counts.
- Top four tower damage-share bars with kills.
- Road heat, failure tags, compact recap, and three prioritized actions.

Campaign-completion results keep the full campaign summary layout and hide the run charts.

## Automated Acceptance

- Eight animated enemy identities and eight animated tower identities are present in the L09 fixture.
- Enemy and tower event motion is active.
- Three audio voices and at least seven distinct cue profiles are initialized.
- Floating feedback stays within the character budget.
- Five score bars, at least three lane bars, and at least three tower bars contain data.
- Result text has no measured overflow at 1280x720 and 960x540.
- P9 presentation, P11.3 visual ordering, P12.0 route/build geometry, and P8.4 campaign completion remain green.
