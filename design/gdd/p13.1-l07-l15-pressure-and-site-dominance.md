# P13.1 L07 / L15 Pressure and Site Dominance

## Goal

Remove avoidable opening damage and abrupt concurrency cliffs while preserving each
level's enemy count, health, and strategic identity. Make site variants produce
different plans without turning one route depth into the mandatory answer.

## L07 Curve

- Wave 1 now presents four runners, then four swarm enemies after a six-second read
  window. Removed kill income is transferred to clear income, so the mission economy
  is unchanged when all enemies are defeated.
- Wave 2 removes one plated enemy and two swarm enemies, also transferring their kill
  income to the wave clear reward.
- Waves 3-7 are expanded from roughly 3-4 seconds to 7.10-7.80 seconds.
- Wave 9 is expanded to 7.50 seconds and wave 18 to 8.70 seconds.
- Enemy counts, enemy health, and total threat are unchanged outside wave 1.

The opening is now a readable targeting test rather than unavoidable integrity tax.
The early exam sequence still escalates total threat, but its groups arrive in
separable tactical beats.

## L15 Curve

- Wave 1 separates runners and carriers across a 4.50-second window.
- Wave 2 separates the titan and swarm across an 8.17-second window.
- Wave 3 is expanded from 4.47 to 6.70 seconds to smooth the 25.5 to 47.8 threat jump.
- Waves 6-9 are expanded to 8.20-11.00 seconds.
- Wave 11 is expanded from 6.47 to 10.70 seconds.
- Wave 12 is expanded from 6.08 to 9.70 seconds.
- Wave 15 is expanded to 11.45 seconds.
- Wave 16 is expanded from 6.66 to 9.18 seconds.
- Wave 17 is expanded to 10.70 seconds.

These changes preserve the exam-level threat totals while turning simultaneous
multi-lane spikes into readable response windows.

## Autoplay Strategy Corrections

Control Lattice keeps its control identity but no longer sends every tower down a
utility branch:

- Frost, Arc, Beacon, and Grav use utility upgrades.
- Rail, Cinder, Siege, and Flak use damage upgrades as finishers.
- The opening establishes area damage, slow coverage, and a Rail finisher.
- The strategy uses a six-to-eight tower soft limit before wave 12 and may expand to
  nine sites afterward, converting the remaining budget into upgrades.

Adaptive Network and Focused Fire retain their existing expansion behavior.

## Site Policy

- Established entrance and exit coverage bonuses are capped at 4.8 and 6.0.
- Site depth preference weights are reduced to 0.25 for the center variant and 0.45
  for the entrance/exit variants.
- Coverage, incremental coverage, counter interception, and control overlap decide
  site quality. Variant depth now acts as a tie-breaker.
- Neutral authored-site quality must have at least 12 candidates and no single site
  may exceed 22% of aggregate neutral quality.
- A completed runtime may not exceed 32% damage contribution from one site.

## Runtime Acceptance

The dedicated matrix runs:

- 2 levels: L07 and L15.
- 3 strategies: Focused Fire, Control Lattice, and Adaptive Network.
- 3 site variants.
- 18 rendered runtime runs at a fixed random seed.

Each level must meet all of the following:

- At least 7 victories out of 9 runs.
- At least 2 victories out of 3 for every strategy.
- At least 2 victories out of 3 for every site variant.
- Site win-rate spread no greater than 33.4 percentage points.
- No run exceeds four opening escapes.
- No run exceeds 32% top-site damage share.
- UI bounds, overlap, text fit, console, P12.4, P13.0, and static P13.1 checks pass.

The intentionally weakest strategy/site pairing may remain a loss when all matrix
constraints pass. This preserves a meaningful bad-plan outcome without allowing one
site or one strategy to become mandatory.
