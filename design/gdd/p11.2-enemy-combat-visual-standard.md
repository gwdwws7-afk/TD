# P11.2 Enemy Readability And Combat Visual Standard

> Status: implemented and MCP-verified
> Scope: enemy silhouettes, threat hierarchy, status icons, eight-tower projectiles, impact feedback

## Product Intent

P11.2 makes combat readable before it makes combat louder. A player should identify the dangerous unit, its pressure type, its current debuffs, and the tower responsible for an attack without pausing or reading floating prose.

Every combat signal combines at least two independent channels. Color communicates identity quickly; shape, thickness, count, motion, and placement preserve meaning at low resolution and under colorblind settings.

## Enemy Readability Stack

Enemy presentation is ordered from persistent to transient:

1. Production enemy silhouette and animation.
2. Synchronized alpha-only outline behind the sprite.
3. Threat category marker for Tactical, Elite, and Boss units.
4. One to four threat pips representing level.
5. Compact status strip for active combat states.
6. Tower-specific impacts and short combat feedback.

Routine enemies keep a thin dark separation edge and no floating threat marker. This prevents swarm waves from becoming a field of icons.

## Threat Levels

| Level | Rule | Outline | Marker behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine | Basic fast, light, or swarm pressure | Thin dark edge | Hidden |
| Tactical | Armor, heavy, support, attrition, spawn, or special pressure | Category-tinted edge | Fixed category marker and two pips |
| Elite | Elite tag or high threat cost | Stronger pulsing edge | Larger marker and three pips |
| Boss | Boss or final tag | Highest contrast pulse | Boss marker and four pips |

Threat categories use six fixed silhouettes: fast chevrons, swarm cluster, armored shield, support network, special diamond, and boss crown. Category colors are cyan, coral, gold, green, violet, and red respectively.

## Status Strip

The status strip is centered above the enemy and reflows without changing enemy layout. It supports five simultaneous states:

| State | Shape | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armor break | Cracked shield | Gold | Flat armor is reduced |
| Slow | Snowflake | Cyan | Movement speed is reduced |
| Stagger | Broken bolt | Orange | Short movement interruption |
| Exposed | Target reticle | Red | Incoming damage is amplified |
| Resonance | Broadcast arcs | Green | Resonance interactions are enabled |

Icons remain the same size and order at 1280x720 and 960x540. Timers remain in simulation code; the world strip communicates active state, not exact duration.

## Feedback Hierarchy

- Tower projectiles own their hit effect. The generic enemy hit animation is reserved for environmental or source-less damage.
- Routine hits are compact and short. Area hits may show one boundary at the real effect diameter.
- Specialist and resonance effects may add one secondary ring, but they do not replace projectile identity.
- Gravity and area boundaries use thin procedural rings rather than enlarged build markers.
- Enemy mechanic telegraphs remain above routine impacts but below boss warnings and critical cinematics.
