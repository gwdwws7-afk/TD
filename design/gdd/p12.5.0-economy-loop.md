# P12.5.0 Economy Loop

## Goal

P12.5.0 keeps money relevant through the final five waves. A successful defense should still choose between an ultimate tower upgrade and an increasingly expensive authored scenario command instead of reaching 12 towers, 36 upgrades and a four-digit idle reserve long before the ending.

Enemy health, wave pressure and map capacity are outside this pass. Economy must create the decision; inflated combat durability must not consume the surplus indirectly.

## Runtime Rules

- Combat bounty pays 58 percent of the enemy catalog reward, then tapers from 55 percent mission progress to an 8 percent final multiplier.
- Wave-clear income remains dependable longer, then tapers from 60 percent mission progress to a 70 percent final multiplier.
- Upgrade tiers cost 0.8x, 1.4x and 4.6x tower build cost. The opening pair establishes a specialization cheaply; the third purchase is the deliberate ultimate commitment.
- Existing scenario commands scale by mission phase up to 1.55x and by repeated use at 22 percent per activation, capped at 1.88x.
- Command base costs are 10 for Signal Gate, 14 for Reserve Train, 12 for Route Switch, 20 for Kiln Purge and 28 for Phase Breaker.
- There is no new currency, shop or permanent economy subsystem.

## Decision Model

The final five waves are evaluated as one spending window. Runtime telemetry records opening and closing budget, combat bounty, wave-clear income, scenario spending, tower construction, upgrades and purchase count for every wave.

A victorious run has economy decision value when:

- ending budget is at most 999;
- at least two purchases occur in the final five waves;
- full build and full upgrades are not reached before the final-five window;
- combat and wave-clear income telemetry are both present.

Defeats retain complete income telemetry but are not required to have five late-wave records when the defense ends earlier.

## Automation

`tools/td_mcp_p1250_economy_gate.ps1` runs the deterministic 20-level x 3-difficulty x 3-strategy matrix and the rendered P12.4 release set. It rejects:

- incomplete or stalled sessions;
- any victorious run without economy decision value;
- saturation before the final five waves;
- the legacy 12-tower, 36-upgrade, 1000-plus reserve state;
- missing economy telemetry;
- fast-matrix economy saturation or early-fortification errors.

Primary artifacts are written under `output/playtest/p1250_release_final`:

- `p1250_economy_audit.json`
- `p1250_economy_report.md`
- `p1250_economy_runs.csv`
- `p124_calibration_index.json`

## Scope Guard

P12.5.0 changes income conversion, upgrade purchasing cadence, command costs, autoplay purchasing policy, simulator parity and telemetry. It does not change enemy HP, enemy wave pressure, tower combat output, pathing or legal build sites.

## Verification

Verified on 2026-07-23: **PASS**. The release gate completed 37/37 rendered Unity sessions with 29 victories, zero economy-decision failures, zero early saturation, zero legacy full-build overflow and zero telemetry failures. Victorious defenses ended with at most 975 budget, made at least two purchases in the final five waves and converted an average 65.6 percent of final-five income into towers, upgrades or scenario commands.

The companion fast matrix completed 180/180 runs with deterministic fingerprint `3E11A2D4`, all five exam gates passing, zero curve alarms and zero economy errors. The retained P12.4 calibration also passes: median score error is 5.9 points, median duration error is 16.48 percent and runtime/simulator victory agreement is 81.8 percent. Enemy HP and wave pressure were unchanged.
