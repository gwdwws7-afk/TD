# P11.3 Tower Foundation, Upgrade, and Ordering Standard

## Goal

Make every tower read as a grounded battlefield object, make legal placement and attack timing legible without oversized overlays, and remove arbitrary enemy-versus-tower draw order.

## Foundation and Placement

- `Art/tower_base_plate` is the shared source for recommended build spots, cursor previews, and occupied tower foundations.
- Empty recommended spots use a neutral low-alpha plate at 68% of one cell.
- The cursor preview uses the same plate at 72% of one cell, with green or red state tint.
- An occupied recommended spot hides its empty marker; the tower foundation remains at 72% of one cell.
- Tower identity comes from the tower silhouette, P11 accent color, projectile language, and upgrade pips rather than a different foundation shape.

## Attack Charge

- A target acquisition starts a short tower-specific windup before projectile release.
- Total shot cadence remains aligned with the configured shots-per-second value by subtracting windup from the following cooldown.
- Charge feedback is a four-segment identity-colored ring under the tower.
- Maximum charge diameter is 58% of one grid cell. It must never become a range indicator.
- Charge feedback disappears when the tower has no target, is cooling down, or combat is over.

## Upgrade Presentation

- Every committed upgrade emits one branch-colored foundation ring, one branch icon, and one tower-identity impact burst.
- Damage upgrades use orange; utility upgrades use cyan.
- One to three persistent diamonds below the foundation show upgrade tier and preserve each committed branch color.
- Existing specialization aura remains reserved for two matching branch choices and is not reused as a generic tier indicator.

## World Ordering

- Map art and props remain below order 10.
- Tower and enemy body order is calculated from world Y in the shared 11-19 band.
- Foundations, shadows, silhouettes, and specialization auras use offsets from their owner body order.
- Projectiles and impact feedback use orders 22-25.
- Upgrade presentation uses order 27 and range preview uses order 30.
- Threat and status overlays remain attached to enemy body order and cannot change physical occlusion.

## Silhouette Integrity

- Enemy and tower animation frames are scanned for large enclosed transparent regions.
- A hole above 8% of the combined solid-plus-hole area is treated as a broken body silhouette.
- Furnace Matriarch's six source frames contain a known 29% center-alpha defect. `TD/FurnaceMatriarchBody` restores a plated chest and furnace core while preserving the animated outer body.
- Small mechanical gaps in Rail Lancer T3, Grav Snare T3, and Arc Welder are intentional and remain below 4%.

## Acceptance

- Eight tower kinds all have active foundations and charge indicators.
- The fixture contains tier 1, 2, and 3 towers with both upgrade branches.
- Recommended spot occupation is visible in the marker audit.
- Enemy and tower body orders span at least two depth values and match their current world Y.
- Projectile order is above every body; range preview is above projectile order.
- 1280x720 and 960x540 screenshots pass UI bounds, overlap, text-fit, P11.3, and console checks.
- The alpha-hole audit scans all enemy and tower frames and reports no unexpected hole above 8%.
