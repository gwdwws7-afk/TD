# P13.3 Combat Readability Finalization

## Goal

Make every combat decision visually trustworthy at shipping scale. Enemies follow the
painted rail centerline, towers occupy authored terrain-safe foundations, and critical
combat information remains readable when eight towers and at least eighteen enemies
share the screen.

## Route Contract

- Every runtime lane uses an authored rail centerline.
- Catmull-Rom output is resampled so adjacent runtime points are no farther than 0.05
  world units apart.
- Current and maximum observed enemy deviation from the active path must remain at or
  below 0.01 world units.
- Forecast route lines are hidden during normal play.
- Enemy sprites face their travel direction and use a restrained turn pose capped at
  4.3 degrees.

## Grounding Contract

- Every map exposes exactly 12 authored build sites.
- A site is legal only when its complete 0.78-unit foundation fits inside the board and
  maintains at least 0.68 units of clearance from every route segment.
- Occupied sites hide the placement marker but retain the tower foundation and shadow.
- The build preview combines the final foundation art with a separate green or red
  legality outline.
- Authored sites avoid the persistent HUD, wave-intel panel, upgrade panel, and bottom
  command strip at the reference viewports, including a 34-pixel interaction margin.

## Interaction Contract

- Hover uses a compact cyan ground ring no wider than 0.76 cell.
- Selection uses a compact amber ground ring no wider than 0.86 cell.
- Full attack range is shown only for an explicitly selected tower or a valid build
  preview. Hover alone never opens the full range disc.
- Build spots, range, preview, and interaction rings render below unit bodies.

## Enemy Information Priority

From lowest to highest:

1. Unit body and foot shadow.
2. Projectile and presentation effects.
3. Resistance and weakness traits.
4. Temporary status effects.
5. Threat marker and boss-critical marker.

Tactical enemies always show a weakness badge. Armored and boss enemies also show a
resistance badge. Armor break, exposed, resonance, slow, and stagger use a stable
five-slot priority order.

## Dense Combat Acceptance

Run `tools/td_mcp_p133_combat_readability_matrix.ps1`.

The gate covers L01, L05, L09, L13, L17, L20, plus 960x540 probes for L09 and L20.
Every probe must pass route, build-site, tower placement, enemy motion, interaction,
trait, status, occlusion, density, UI bounds, UI overlap, text fit, and console checks.
