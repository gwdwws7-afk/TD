# P13.4 Audio, Visual, and Input Feel

## Goal

Make combat outcomes readable by weight and consequence while preserving a clean
board at high enemy density. Every tower must communicate its identity before,
during, and after a shot. Mouse, keyboard, and gamepad must reach the same battle
commands without ambiguous focus.

## Combat Feedback Hierarchy

| Signal | Tier | Visual rule | Audio rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hit | Routine | Damage only, short lifetime | Light tower-dependent impact |
| Critical hit | Tactical | Gold `CRIT` marker | High rising strike |
| Armor break | Tactical | Amber break marker | Low metallic fracture |
| Slow | Tactical | Cyan speed marker | Controlled falling tone |
| Specialization | Tactical/Critical | Magenta identity marker | Branch-dependent accent |
| Resonance | Tactical/Critical | Green sync marker | Harmonic command tone |
| Boss damage | Tactical | Orange `BOSS` marker | Low heavy impact |
| Leak | Critical | Red breach marker and short cinematic | Dedicated alarm channel |

Critical hit is deterministic. It is produced by a damage specialist matching a
counter/mark, or by a hit worth at least 14% of a non-boss target's maximum
health. Boss hits use their own signal and do not flood the critical-hit layer.

## Short Cinematics

- Wave transition: dispatch and clear.
- Dangerous lane: calculated from the actual wave group counts and lane share.
- Boss phase: entry, phase escalation, or a cancelled phase.
- Defense breach: leak and critical line integrity.

The cinematic surface clamps duration to 1.35 seconds. It uses a short marker
punch, stripe sweep, and directional settle without pausing combat or covering
the bottom command area.

## Eight-Tower Identity

| Tower | Charge rhythm | Projectile language | Impact shape | Upgrade motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rail Lancer | rail lock | needle bolt | linear pierce | rivet rise |
| Cinder Mortar | mortar fuse | weighted shell | ember bloom | furnace lift |
| Frost Coil | cryo condense | crystal shard | crystal cross | cryo lock |
| Arc Welder | arc stutter | chain spark | electric fork | spark snap |
| Siege Drill | siege spool | heavy bore | hex break | ram lock |
| Ember Flak | flak redline | burst pellet | star burst | redline pop |
| Resonance Beacon | beacon sync | signal orbit | concentric pulse | relay rise |
| Grav Snare | grav compress | gravity orbit | implosion disc | singularity fold |

Charge circles remain at or below 0.62 world units. Identity comes from timing,
rotation, segment order, recoil, projectile behavior, and impact shape rather
than oversized selection or charge rings.

## Input Contract

Mouse:

- Left click selects or builds.
- Right click upgrades with the selected branch.
- UI buttons expose the complete command set.

Keyboard:

- `1` to `8`: tower slot.
- `Q` / `E`: damage or utility branch.
- `U`: apply the selected upgrade to the focused tower.
- `Z` / `X`: resonance command.
- `Space`: start wave.
- `P`: pause.
- `-` / `+`: speed.
- `Esc`: settings.

Gamepad:

- D-pad/left stick: UI navigation with automatic focus recovery.
- South: submit focused command.
- East: back.
- North: start wave.
- West: scenario command.
- Start: pause.
- Select: settings.
- Shoulders: speed down/up.

The D-pad is navigation-only. It does not silently activate upgrade or resonance
commands while focus is moving.

## Effect Budgets

| Budget | Limit |
|---|---:|
| Floating combat signals | 12 |
| Hit signals | 4 |
| Other signals per kind | 2 |
| Routine world effects | 18 |
| Tactical world effects | 12 |
| Accent world effects | 8 |
| Total world effects | 32 |
| Maximum signal duration | 1.05 s |
| Maximum world-effect duration | 0.90 s |
| Maximum signal/effect alpha | 0.96 |

When the budget is full, routine effects are suppressed first. Tactical and
critical signals may replace an older routine signal, but never exceed the hard
cap.

## Acceptance

Run:

```powershell
.\tools\td_mcp_p134_audio_visual_input_matrix.ps1 -RefreshScripts
```

Required:

- Eight feedback types and four cinematic types observed.
- Eight unique charge, projectile, impact, and upgrade identities.
- All 20 required audio clips and three mix sources available.
- Mouse, keyboard, gamepad navigation, focus, and shortcuts pass.
- World effects never exceed 32; combat signals never exceed 12.
- 1280x720 and 960x540 probes have no bounds, overlap, text-fit, or Console
  failures.
- P9, P11.3, and P13.3 regressions remain green.
