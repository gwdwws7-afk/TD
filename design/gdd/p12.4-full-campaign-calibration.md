# P12.4 Full Campaign Calibration

## Goal

P12.4 calibrates the complete campaign against rendered Unity play rather than relying on fast-rules outcomes alone. The target is a readable three-difficulty curve, multiple viable formations, build sites whose value changes with route and scenario state, and a result screen that can explain why a defense failed.

## Evidence Layers

- The deterministic fast-rules matrix scans all 20 levels, three difficulties and three strategy profiles for cliffs, flat regions, inversions, stalls and strategy collapse.
- Rendered MCP sessions replay representative campaign progression at L01, L05, L09, L13, L17 and L20. The runtime profile includes earned chapter rewards, level-based starting resources and permanent tower power.
- Release scope runs Standard with all three strategy profiles, Veteran and Ember Trial anchors, the L14 cliff probe, and alternate-site A/B variants at L09 and L20.
- Runtime analytics record wave reached, first leak, route pressure, site and tower contribution, completion time, economy remainder, score and three prioritized replay recommendations.

Fast-rules results are a forecasting tool. A curve is accepted only when its direction agrees with complete rendered runs and duration drift remains bounded.

## Calibration Changes

- Generated maps expose 12 safe near-route sites with spacing and clearance checks. Hollow Kiln and Last Ember use authored 12-site layouts so late exams cannot be decided by an accidental shortage of legal placements.
- Site scoring samples the entire road polyline, including entrance and exit coverage. Prep AI expands with wave count and active lane count, then reacts to uncovered route segments.
- Campaign progression now applies level-scaled starting budget, chapter-scaled integrity and 2.5 percent tower power per cleared level to both runtime and simulator rules.
- Adaptive formations cap repeated swarm and armor responses before they crowd out the third tower identity. The controller deliberately selects a less-used valid counter after the cap.
- Opening formations establish armor, swarm and speed answers before repeating a tower. Uncovered route checks use the true path endpoints; the first tower retains central efficiency, then later towers receive a one-time priority for every unprotected exit.
- L05 receives one additional opening tower's reserve and a lighter health surcharge. L13 removes its redundant health stack. L14 becomes a front-loaded defense with scarce later salvage: a 16 -> 29 -> 29 opening pressure ramp, additional deployment budget and 12 points of temporary line depth.
- Post-victory validation excludes a chapter reward claimed after deployment, keeping the current run's expected and actual rules on the same progression snapshot.
- Defeat advice selects a leaked or unresolved road segment before falling back to generic heat, so the first recommendation points to a concrete coverage problem.
- The fast simulator records opening, minimum, average and late capacity ratios. Timed reinforcement, short-reaction environment and final Boss mechanics use those ratios to predict survival; score remains responsible for grade and five-axis quality rather than acting as the only victory switch.

## Release Matrix

Run:

```powershell
./tools/td_mcp_p124_full_campaign_calibration.ps1 -Scope Release -OutputRoot output/playtest/p124_release_v1
```

The release matrix contains 37 complete Unity sessions:

- 18 Standard runs: six anchors by three strategy profiles.
- 12 high-difficulty runs: six Veteran and six Ember Trial adaptive anchors.
- Three additional L14 Ember Trial strategy probes.
- Four alternate-site variants across L09 and L20; their base runs are shared with the anchor set.

Before rendered sessions start, the wrapper retries the 180-run fast matrix up to three times. Every runtime session must produce a complete JSON record, clean Console evidence, valid UI/text audits and a populated three-item analytics recap.

## Acceptance Gates

- All scheduled real sessions complete without stalls; UI, text-fit and Console audits pass.
- Fast-rules matrix completes 180/180 with zero curve alarms and five passing chapter exams.
- Runtime and simulator victory agreement is at least 80 percent.
- Mean score error is at most 8 points and mean calibrated duration error is at most 20 percent for comparable complete victories.
- No tower kind contributes more than 78 percent when the run has meaningful tower choice.
- No build site contributes more than 58 percent; repeated top-site frequency is at most 75 percent once a map has six or more samples.
- L09 and L20 site A/B groups each contain at least three samples and expose at least two distinct top-site loadouts through a changed site or assigned tower kind.
- Every completed result contains three precise recommendations tied to observed leaks, route pressure, economy or contribution concentration.

A completed run with all 12 towers, all 36 upgrades and more than 1000 unspent budget is reported as an economy-saturation warning. It is intentionally a soft P12.5 economy-sink signal rather than a reason to distort combat difficulty.

## Follow-On Boundary

P12.4 changes combat numbers, progression rules, placement eligibility and diagnostic precision. It does not add a new tower, enemy or map. Standalone performance, save recovery, crash handling, production identity and release packaging remain P12.5 work.
